## Study scopes and selection

Historical `lower-manhattan-window-v1` and all 24 schematic segments are unchanged: bbox [-74.012,40.710,-74.000,40.720]. Official `lower-manhattan-pilot-v2` is [-74.020,40.700,-73.985,40.725]; shoreline context [-74.030,40.695,-73.980,40.730]. These are chosen application extents, not official neighborhood boundaries or survey-accuracy claims.

LION acquisition requests all intersecting Manhattan regular-street rows (`FeatureTyp='0'`, LBoro or RBoro=1) through fixed ArcGIS query parameters and sorted object-ID batches. Pavement and shoreline use Socrata geometry intersection against the specified rectangle. Keep complete source features, not clipped lines or a first-N sample. Caps: 10,000 roads, 20,000 permits, 10,000 rows for each other source; exceedance blocks acquisition/publication. API viewport limit 2,000 is separate.

Permit acquisition is deliberately bounded by borough **MANHATTAN**, issue datetime ≥explicit `--permit-since`, and exact on-street names present in the selected LION roads, partitioned into sets of 20 names. Initial cutoff **2026-10-01**. It includes distant parts of long streets and can omit aliases/missing-name records; it is **not complete Lower Manhattan geographic permit coverage**, nor all permits still in effect. All initially imported permits have unknown geometry and no accepted road link: scope membership is unverified_candidate. Records remain inspectable rather than inventing locations. Source-wide missing-geography records outside the documented query cannot be claimed covered.

This is a reviewed local-inspection import, not blanket redistribution/production clearance.

Source revisions expose agency attribution, terms links/review version, null license identifier and DCP's published use/accuracy disclaimer. Future redistribution/product deployment needs its own applicable terms review; do not relabel this evidence as municipal approval.

## Provenance, matching and refresh

Fixed-origin HTTPS acquisition uses system `/usr/bin/curl` with certificate verification, no redirects, 30-second attempt deadlines, two bounded transient retries, 25 MB per response and explicit row caps. JSON/API errors, HTML-as-data, missing/oversize/truncated responses and schema/update drift fail before publication. No archives are expanded; dictionaries remain XLSX evidence. Source URLs are not a CLI parameter. No startup/background imports.

Offline stage verifies physical source/metadata/dictionary/terms files, matches raw rows to downloaded pages, normalizes typed records and quarantines invalid rows. Source revisions retain canonical raw-record-set hash, page/dictionary/schema checksums, query, source/update/CRS/cadence/terms and adapter. Acquisition/retrieval, source update and inspection/permit timestamps remain distinct. Dry-run reports have counts/hash/quarantine and no database writes. Reviewed report hash gates atomic publication; nonzero rejects additionally require --allow-rejects. New snapshots never update/delete previous sources, records, links or saved synthetic calculations. See DATA_MODEL for identity and ARCHITECTURE for verified backup/recovery.

OFT is documented as three concatenated 6-digit on/from/to street codes, preserving leading zeros. Exact equal OFT creates inspectable DOT same-location groups, not a confirmed LION segment/direction relationship. No invented SegmentID↔OFT conversion is made. With geometry, projected EPSG:32618 minimum distance ≤5 m generates candidates; 5 m is a screening tolerance, **not accuracy or physical conflict**. Unlocated OFT can generate candidates on LION StreetCode equal to the first component; this may include many blocks. No name-only, nearest, unique, parallel/grade-separated or OFT-prefix candidate becomes accepted automatically. Review requires eligible target, complete source-record hash, reviewer and reason; changed source content invalidates stale reviews. Initial snapshot has zero accepted links. Full candidate evidence remains available.

Unknown utility coordinates/depths, work dates, cost, traffic and budgets stay unknown. Official records never enter legacy WorkProject/estimate/scenario/AI adapters. After publication reads are offline and independent of source availability. API errors never activate synthetic fallback. Historical source/fixture details below refer only to M1–M6.

## M2 synthetic planning catalog (`demo-m2-v1`)

`data/demo/maintenance.json` and `maintenance-manifest.json` add five related road assets, 26 assessment records (18 undated M1 summaries plus eight dated demo records), and three repair options to the unchanged `demo-m1-v1` geometry snapshot. Stable road IDs: R-001 poor 100×8 m; R-002 good 120×7 m; R-003 fair 150×8 m; R-004 unknown dimensions/surface/assessment; R-005 poor 900×12 m. Every number/date is synthetic and each source is `demo-maintenance-assumptions-v1` or the original M1 source. No geometric distance is calculated. The 10,800 m² case illustrates large cost differences and cannot establish which treatment is feasible.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preventive | 6.00 | 4.80 / 7.80 | 1,500 / 500 | 20% | 1,200 | 1 / 0 |

Bundled rates include materials/labor/equipment; mobilization and traffic allowances are additional and no crew/resource costs are added again. Utility relocation, drainage redesign, permits, taxes and design fees are excluded. Quantities assume full rectangular scope; structural condition, layer design, actual resources, traffic phasing, weekends/weather and subsurface information are unverified. Low/high vary only the bundled unit rate by −20%/+30%; this is an authored sensitivity assumption, not calibrated uncertainty. Curing/setup/productivity are illustrative, not a contractor schedule. Basis date 2026-11-07 identifies an authored rate version, not market research.

No reviewed external construction-price payload was available in the repository. M2 intentionally uses no official price claim or inferred accuracy. `scripts/create_m2_fixture.py` reproduces exact JSON and SHA-256 manifest bytes; seed verifies counts, IDs, references and checksums before transactional insert. A changed manifest cannot overwrite an imported fixture. The prior DEMO_PLAN's scenario rates/arithmetic remain illustrative future M4 fixtures, not this M2 catalog. Future scenarios must select and freeze an explicit consistent rate/scope basis rather than assume that earlier narrative is implemented.
